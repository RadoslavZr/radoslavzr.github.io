Terms of use

IS MUNI Calendar is a personal study-calendar utility maintained by RadoslavZr.

Personal use

The connector is configured privately for its operator's student and Google accounts. There is no public registration, paid service or subscription offered by this project. Only authorize a Google account you are entitled to use.

Study dates and availability

Events reflect the source information found and confirmed by the study event system. Source pages can change, collection can fail, and some deadlines may not be found. Verify important dates, registrations and submission requirements directly with the university or course teacher. A calendar entry does not register you for an exam or submit coursework.

Synchronization and reminders depend on the private server, Google's services, valid authorization and device settings. Continuous availability and notification delivery are not guaranteed.

Calendar changes and stopping use

The connector creates and maintains its dedicated IS MUNI calendar. It can update source dates and remove entries following confirmed cancellations or your configured exclusions. You can stop synchronization, revoke Google access, or delete the calendar. Revoking access does not automatically remove previously created events or server records; see the privacy policy.
